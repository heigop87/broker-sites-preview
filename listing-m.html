Understand the Asking Price

Price Value Factors

What moves the price of a boat like this one.
Year and engine hours
A 2022 boat with 260 hours sits early in its life. Hours and service records move price more than the year alone.
Engines and drives
Twin Volvo Penta IPS. Drive type, warranty left and the last service date all count.
Equipment
Stabilisers, electronics, tender and toys can add or take away a large share of the asking price.
Condition and refits
Survey results, paint, teak and soft goods. Recent work shows up in the price.
Location and season
Boats in South Florida in winter draw more buyers. Tax and duty status also changes the cost to you.
